VisManager Web

Review a folder of images, PDFs and cube files, mark what to keep, and export the result. Files are read as copies — nothing on your disk is ever modified or deleted.

Getting files in

Use Open folder or drag a folder onto the window. The whole tree is read, so subfolders come too. Loading a second folder asks whether to add it to the current review or replace it; adding keeps the marks and notes you have already made.

Reviewing

Every file starts as KEEP. Mark the ones you do not want as DELETE — that only excludes them from the exports and lists them in the delete list, it does not touch the originals. Flag marks a file for follow-up and Note attaches text to it; the Notes button in the toolbar lists everything flagged and jumps to a file when you click it. Remove takes a file out of the review entirely, as though it had not been loaded.

Continuous moves into the next folder when you reach the end of one; Wrap stays inside the current folder.

Cube files

Cubes render as isosurfaces with a ball-and-stick structure. Drag to orbit, right-drag (or Shift-drag) to move the model, scroll to zoom. The isovalue starts at 5% of the peak amplitude and the slider is logarithmic, because cube values span orders of magnitude.

Lock pins the current isovalue, colours and camera angle to that file. Locked files reopen exactly as you left them, and batch export renders them from the pinned angle instead of a default one — worth setting when a particular view shows a lobe clearly. Snapshot exports just the cube on screen, at the resolution chosen in the export dialog.

Settings… covers opacity, lobe and background colours, per-element atom colours, structure scale, hydrogen and bond toggles, surface smoothing, bond tolerance, and graphics quality. The panel can be dragged by its title bar and resized from its corner.

Exporting

The export dialog can write a ZIP of the kept files, a combined PDF, one PDF per folder or per file, a notes report, a delete list, and PNG snapshots of kept cubes at 1x to 4x. Per folder you can convert kept images to PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF. Use All and None to set the outputs quickly.

To act on the delete list, run it yourself — it ships with the exact command for macOS, Linux and PowerShell.

Layout

Drag the divider beside the folder list to resize it, and the bar above the buttons to resize the control panel; dragging that one small switches the buttons to a compact row. Expand gives the viewer the whole window, F11 goes fullscreen. Folders in the sidebar expand to show their files, and the list takes arrow keys when it has focus.

Shortcuts

Every shortcut is printed on its button. Click Shortcuts in the toolbar to rebind any of them; they are stored in this browser.

Formats

PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP and ICO render natively, PDF through pdf.js, TGA through a built-in decoder, and Gaussian/ORCA cube files in 3D. TIFF and DDS are listed and can be marked, but not previewed.
